Project 2

We visited a local school and taught students the basics of coding and AI in a simple, interactive way.

Project Description

In this project, our team organized a short workshop at a local school. We introduced students to beginner coding concepts like logic, conditions, and simple algorithms. We also explained what artificial intelligence is, where it is used, and how students can start learning AI in the future.

Main Topics: Coding basics, algorithms, AI introduction

Goal: Inspire younger students to explore technology
